Commonquestions.

Should I talk to the police without a lawyer?

You are generally not required to answer investigators' questions, and you can ask for a lawyer first. What you say can be used against you, so speak with counsel before agreeing to any interview.

I was just arrested. What should I do first?

Stay calm, do not discuss the facts with anyone but your attorney, and ask for a lawyer. Write down what you remember while it is fresh, and keep every document you were given.

How soon should I call?

As soon as you can. Deadlines for bail hearings, license suspensions and subpoena responses can be short, and early advice can protect options that are hard to recover later.

Is my first conversation confidential?

Communications with a lawyer made to obtain legal advice are generally privileged. Submitting a web form does not by itself create an attorney-client relationship; the firm confirms that in writing.
